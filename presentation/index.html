✦ How It Works

The Booking Pipeline

From launch to confirmation — every step the bot executes automatically.

1

Burst Wait

Smart delay calculation. Wakes up 10s before target time, enters 2–3s burst polling mode for 150s.

2

Login

Navigates to Goethe CAS login, enters credentials, handles MFA if prompted. Retries with exponential backoff.

3

Navigate Exam

Selects the correct exam level (A1/A2/B1), location (Karachi/Lahore), and date. Handles dynamic page transitions.

4

Poll Slot

Monitors the exact time slot in burst mode. Refreshes every 2–3 seconds until the slot appears, then clicks immediately.

5

Submit Booking

Fills booking form, handles CAPTCHA if configured, submits. Retries on failure with smart backoff.

6

Confirm

Verifies booking confirmation page. Sends success notification via Telegram and Email. Logs completion.
